Our Story

More Than an NGO —
A Movement

She Can Foundation was born from a simple truth: when women are given real support — not pity, not charity, but genuine opportunity — they rise, and they lift entire communities with them.

Since 2016, we've worked in schools, homes, and workplaces to dismantle barriers and replace them with confidence, skills, and community.

📚
Education
Skills workshops, scholarships, and digital literacy for every stage of life.
🤝

💼
Economic Power
Micro-grants, business training, and financial independence programs.
🌱
Wellbeing
Mental health, physical health, and holistic care for every woman.

Voices

Their Words,
Our Purpose

The most honest measure of our work is what the women themselves say.

"

Before She Can, I believed my dreams had an expiry date. This program showed me that the only expiry date was the one I had set in my own mind. Today I run my own tailoring business and employ three other women.

Kavitha Reddy
Entrepreneur · Rise & Earn Program, 2022
"

The mentors here didn't talk at me — they sat with me. They understood what it meant to be first-generation, to carry the weight of expectation. For the first time, I didn't feel alone in my ambition.

Aisha Banu
Software Engineer · She Leads Program, 2023
"

I came into this program unable to speak English or use a computer. Eighteen months later, I am the digital literacy trainer for my village. She Can didn't just teach me skills — it gave me back my voice.

Sunita Devi
Community Trainer · Skills Without Borders, 2021
